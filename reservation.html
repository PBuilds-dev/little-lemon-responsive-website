Reservation Policies

Large Parties

For parties of 8 or more guests contact us directly by phone to make your reservation. Large-party reservations cannot be accommodated through our online booking form.

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

If you need to cancel or modify your reservation, please notify us least ONE HOUR in advance. This allows us to accommodate other guests and prepare accordingly.

Reservation Duration

To ensure a smooth dining experience for all our guests, tables are reserved for a maximum of 60 minutes. We kindly ask that guests arrive promptly for their reservation.

Pet Policy

For the comfort and safety of all our guests, pets are not permitted inside the restaurant. Service animals are welcome in accordance with applicable regulations.

Alfresco Seating

Alfresco seating is weather dependent and may be changed or moved indoors depending on availability and safety conditions.

Service Charge & Deposit

A 10% service charge is applied to all dining experiences. As our dishes are prepared fresh and made to order, this charge reflects the care and personalized service we provide to every guest.

A $50 deposit is required to secure your reservation. The deposit is non-refundable but will be deducted from your final bill on the day of your visit.
